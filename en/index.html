Research Profile

I am a PhD candidate in Materials Science and Engineering at Monash University (2027QS:31), supported by a full scholarship. My work combines AI for Science, machine-learning-assisted materials discovery, high-throughput computation, DFT and molecular dynamics with experimental validation. Application areas include zero-carbon hydrogen production, methane pyrolysis for carbon materials, alloy catalysis, and MXene electrocatalytic nitrate reduction to ammonia. I have published eight first- or co-first-author journal papers; my work has received over 1,000 Google Scholar citations. I have filed 10 invention and other IP applications, four granted.
